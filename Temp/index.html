STUDENT GUIDE • RECEPTION SERVICE

How to claim your lost item

Secondary items are safely stored at the Secondary Reception Desk (Building A, 1st Floor). Here is the step-by-step handover procedure:

1

Find your item on FOUND@ANS

Browse the items list or type keywords into the AI search bar. Check the photos, found date, and location.

Remember or screenshot the Item ID (e.g. LF-2026-002).
2

Visit Secondary Reception

Go to the Secondary Reception Desk in Main Building A between 07:30 and 16:30 (Monday – Friday).

Tell the duty receptionist or Kru Jane the Item ID you found on the website.

3

Verify your ownership

To prevent fraudulent claims, authorities will ask you to verify the item is truly yours using one of these methods:

Student ID Card Matches the name tag written on the item.
Device Unlock Unlocking your phone or tablet screen in person.
Secret Detail Describing unphotographed charms, stickers, or contents.
4

Item released & archived

The authority signs off the digital log, crosses off the reception paper sheet, and hands your item back to you!

Claim Deadline Policy:

Items are stored for 30–45 days. High-value items remain in safe custody; non-sensitive uniform and bottles are donated to charity if unclaimed after the deadline.

AUTHORITY PROTOCOL • KRU JANE & RECEPTION

Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

Official protocol for logging, safeguarding, and releasing lost property at Secondary Reception.

1. Physical Sheet Intake

When someone hands in an item:

  • Have the finder fill the paper clipboard log (Date, Room, Finder Name).
  • Record the Sheet Ref No. (e.g. Sheet #12 Row 4).
  • Inspect for student names or student card numbers.

2. Digital Registration & Privacy

  • Click "Add Found Item" to upload a clear photo.
  • Write detailed Public Description for students.
  • Fill Private Verification Notes (e.g. lockscreen wallpaper, 50 baht inside, chipped corner). This is completely hidden from students!
  • Toggle High-Value for phones, laptops, wallets, jewellery.

3. Handover Verification

  • Verify student ID card or have the student unlock the phone screen.
  • Click "Claim" on the portal to record the student name, ID, and verification notes.
  • Item is immediately archived off active student search.

4. Monday Email Digest

Every Monday morning at 07:00, an automated bulletin is broadcast to all secondary students & staff featuring high-value and recently turned-in items.
